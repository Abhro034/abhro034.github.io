I led development of PlantLife365, a plant health monitoring web application built around low-cost IoT sensing. My work covered sensor system design, front-end architecture, project scope, and business planning. The project won first place in the DAIS Mobile/Web App Challenge at the IISE Annual Conference & Expo 2026.
