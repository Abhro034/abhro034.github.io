I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Auburn University. My research focuses on machine learning for physiological signals, especially sleep staging and sleep biomarker discovery. I also work on computer-aided diagnosis and low-cost sensing for precision agriculture.

## Research interests

- Sleep informatics and polysomnographic signal analysis
- Deep learning and transferability for biosignal classification
- Computer-aided diagnosis and multimodal pattern recognition
- IoT systems and precision agriculture

## Current work

At Auburn, I am a graduate teaching assistant for Python Programming and Database. My recent research has included sleep biomarker discovery in ADHD, automated sleep-stage analysis, 3D CT-based head and neck cancer analysis, and multimodal agricultural sensing.

See my [publications](/publications/), [projects](/portfolio/), and [CV](/cv/) for details. You can also find my work on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MpusZEEAAAAJ&hl=en) and [GitHub](https://github.com/Abhro034).
